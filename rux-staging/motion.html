A modern style shaped by Kyiv circus training.

01

Yuri Pozdnyakov

Community and trade histories identify Pozdnyakov as an influential teacher in the modern Russian-ball tradition; archival certainty is treated carefully.

02

Vladik Miagkostupov

A major performer associated with early commercial dissemination of the modern prop style and documented IJA competition success.

03

Viktor Kee

Ukrainian-born, Kyiv circus-school trained, and important to theatrical body-oriented ball manipulation on international stages.

04

Alexander Koblikov

Known for extraordinary multiplex vocabulary and very high ball counts, helping expand the visual language of contemporary ball juggling.
