02 / WHY IT STOPS

Gravity finishes the catch.

A Russian ball is a hollow sphere with a partial mobile fill. After contact, that fill settles toward the lowest point. The combined center of mass shifts downward, which is why the prop excels when the trick needs to end in a stall, balance or dead landing.

NO MAGIC. NO “EASIER JUGGLING” CLAIM. JUST A DIFFERENT MASS SYSTEM.

04 / PTIV · RUX VAULT

History you can watch.

Partially filled balancing balls predate the modern category. The contemporary style is strongly associated with Soviet/Ukrainian circus-school practice centered on Kyiv, with community histories pointing to teacher Yuri Pozdnyakov, commercial dissemination by Vladik Miagkostupov, international exposure through Viktor Kee, and later technical influence from Alexander Koblikov.
